# 001 — BRD process: hybrid Athanasios recon + HVE @brd-builder

- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Decided by:** Steve (routing advice from Sage)

## Decision
The first BRD is a **partner-facing Hardware BRD** for the IoT hardware partner (layers 1–3: Physical, Instrument, Connect/edge). It is capability-based, not a bill of materials.

It runs in two steps:
1. **Athanasios** reads the Dutch tender documents through `onedrive/` (read-only) and writes an English summary of hardware-relevant requirements to `4. Solution/Hardware Solution/recon/tender-hardware-recon.md`.
2. **HVE `@brd-builder`** (VS Code Copilot Chat, HVE Core 3.3.101) writes the BRD to `4. Solution/Hardware Solution/alliander-schakellokalen-hardware-brd.md`, using Steve's prompt (`4. Solution/Hardware Solution/prompts/hardware-brd-prompt.md`) with the recon summary as the main reference.
3. The Word version is copied to OneDrive `4. Solution/Hardware Solution/` for the bid team. The repo mirrors the OneDrive folder structure.

## Why
- The tender documents are Dutch procurement PDFs. An English summary first gives `@brd-builder` clean input and lets us trace requirements back to the RFP.
- `@brd-builder` gives a structured, resumable BRD workflow that Superteam doesn't have.
- The tender documents stay read-only. Nothing is written to `onedrive/`.
