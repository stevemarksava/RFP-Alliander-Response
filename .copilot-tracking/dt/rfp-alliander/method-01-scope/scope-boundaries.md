# Method 1 — Scope Boundaries (rfp-alliander)

Lo-fi capture. Conversational, not a formal requirements document.

## In Scope (per tender + BRD, document-confirmed)

- Supplier designs, builds, implements, manages, and maintains a digital control layer across 4 initial environments (Haarlem, Zevenaar).
- Hardware partner mandate: layers 1-3 (instrumentation + edge), largely per the 2023/2025 Avanade concept.
- Avanade/Alliander mandate: layers 4-6 (digital twin, AI/agentic, experience).
- Offline-capable edge operation (tender minimum: survive temporary outage per PERF-05; Avanade design choice: fully offline-capable).
- Framework agreement, 3 years + up to 5 x 1-year extensions, scalable to future training locations.

## Out of Scope (document-confirmed)

- Physical construction of schakellokalen/meetvelden.
- Measurement equipment (meetapparatuur) itself.
- Training content / didactic learning lines.
- Archipel replacement (separate tender).

## Known vs. Assumed — Scope Evolution

- **Known (tender text):** MVP scope as written in the 2026 Aanbestedingsleidraad and Bijlage L.
- **Known (lived relationship, not yet cross-checked against tender text):** the original 2023 concept had a *broader* scope that could include Microsoft Teams integration and a "Leerling Volg Systeem" (learner-tracking system). Unconfirmed how much of that broader scope survived into the 2026 MVP tender.
- **Assumption to validate:** that the 2026 tender is a faithful subset of the 2023/2025 concept rather than a reframed problem. The bid team's own open-items list flags this as unconfirmed.

## Flagged Constraint — Prior Involvement (not a DT-scope item to solve, but material to the engagement)

Avanade originated this concept (Feb 2023), received explicit client preference ("we set the benchmark"), and Steven personally authored the official NvI1 clarification questions submitted during the live 2026 procurement. Dutch/EU procurement rules (Aanbestedingswet 2012, implementing Directive 2014/24/EU Art. 41) require contracting authorities to take measures preventing distortion of competition when a bidder had prior involvement in preparing a tender. This has not been confirmed as raised with Alliander or Avanade legal/compliance.

This sits outside Method 1's stakeholder/problem-scoping work but is recorded here because it bounds what the team can safely do next (e.g., how much further informal Alliander contact is advisable before this is resolved).
